School Streams & College Guide

Explore in-depth stream comparisons after 10th and key competitive entrance exams after 12th to land admissions in top colleges.

🚀

Science (PCM - Physics, Chem, Math)

Engineering, AI, Tech & Defense

Ideal for students with analytical mindset interested in software, artificial intelligence, robotics, aerospace, architecture, and armed forces.

Top Degrees: B.Tech / B.E, B.Arch, BCA, B.Sc Computer Science, NDA
Major Exams: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, BITSAT, NDA, CUET
🩺

Science (PCB - Physics, Chem, Bio)

Medicine, Healthcare & Biotech

Best for careers in healthcare, surgery, clinical diagnostics, pharmaceuticals, genetics, nursing, veterinary, and biotechnology research.

Top Degrees: MBBS, BDS, BAMS, B.Pharm, B.Sc Biotech, Nursing
Major Exams: NEET-UG, AIIMS, ICAR, CUET
📈

Commerce (with/without Math)

Finance, Business & Accounting

For students interested in business management, banking, chartered accountancy, corporate finance, equity analysis, and economic policy.

Top Degrees: B.Com (Hons), BBA, BMS, CA, CS, CMA, CFA
Major Exams: IPMAT (IIMs), CA Foundation, CUET-UG, SET
⚖️

Arts & Humanities

Law, Civil Services, Design & Media

Focuses on critical thinking, policy-making, legal frameworks, media, public relations, design, psychology, and public administration (UPSC).

Top Degrees: BA LLB (5-yr Law), B.Des, BA Psychology, Journalism
Major Exams: CLAT (NLUs), CUET-UG, NID DAT, NIFT, AILET
